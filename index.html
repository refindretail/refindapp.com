Trust & safety

Built to protect every transaction

From your first swipe to package delivered, ReFind keeps buyers and sellers safe at every step.

🔒

Secure Payments

Every transaction runs through Stripe. Your card details never touch our servers — industry-standard encryption from checkout to payout.

🛡️

Buyer Protection

Received something that doesn't match the listing? Open a dispute within 3 days and our team steps in to make it right.

Reporting & Blocking

Flag suspicious listings or users in one tap. Block anyone you don't want to interact with — instantly, no explanation required.

Image Moderation

AI scans every listing photo before it goes live. Inappropriate or misleading images are flagged and reviewed automatically.
