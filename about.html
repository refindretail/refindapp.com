About ReFind

ReFind is a product of ReFind Retail, LLC

Who we are

ReFind is a mobile marketplace for buying and selling pre-loved clothing — built around fit, trust, and real offers. We believe secondhand fashion should feel as seamless as buying new, and that every closet has pieces worth giving a second life.

ReFind is developed and operated by ReFind Retail, LLC, a company based in North Carolina.

ReFind Retail, LLC is the legal entity behind the ReFind app and this website. All terms, privacy commitments, and business correspondence fall under ReFind Retail, LLC.

What makes ReFind different

Most secondhand apps let you browse endlessly without knowing if anything will actually fit. ReFind flips that — your measurements come first. Every listing you see is already filtered to your size and body type, so you're only browsing things that can realistically work for you.

When you find something you love, you can make a real offer, message the seller directly, and get it shipped with a label generated in the app. No external links, no "dm for price" — the whole transaction lives inside ReFind.

Our values

Sustainability. Extending the life of clothing is one of the most impactful things individuals can do for the environment. We're building tools that make it easy — and worth it financially — to keep clothes circulating instead of ending up in landfills.

Fairness. Sellers keep the majority of every sale. Our platform fee covers payment processing, shipping infrastructure, and keeping the marketplace safe — not extracting margin from people clearing out their closets.

Trust. Every seller has a public rating. Every listing goes through image moderation. Buyers can make offers, ask questions, and read reviews before committing. We want every transaction to feel safe on both sides.
