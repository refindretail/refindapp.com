Discovery

A feed built around you, not the algorithm

Your feed is personalized to your style preferences, favorite categories, and actual size — not just what's trending. Swipe through items that could genuinely be yours.

  • Swipe right to save to your Wishlist
  • Swipe up to add straight to cart
  • Filter by category, price, condition, and fit
  • Feed learns from your preferences over time
📐

Fit Matching

Know it fits before you buy it

Forget size charts that vary by brand. ReFind compares actual garment measurements to your body measurements and shows you what will actually fit — not just what's labeled your size.

  • Sellers provide pit-to-pit, waist, hips, and length
  • "Fits You" badge on listings within your range
  • Toggle the fit filter to only see matching items
  • Dramatically reduces returns and disappointment
💬

Negotiation

Make an offer. Counter it. Get the deal.

See something you love but the price isn't right? Send an offer. Sellers can accept, decline, or counter — all within the app. No bots, no auction pressure, no millisecond declines.

  • Full offer/counteroffer back-and-forth in messaging
  • Buy Now if you want it at the listed price
  • Transparent — no hidden fees or auction pressure
  • 13% platform fee only charged to sellers on completed sales
📦

Shipping

Prepaid labels, real tracking, no hassle

Sellers generate prepaid shipping labels right in the app. Buyers see exact shipping costs before checkout — no surprises. Tracking updates go to both sides automatically.

  • USPS, UPS, FedEx, and DHL supported via Shippo
  • Tracking updates pushed to buyer and seller
  • Sellers can also arrange their own shipping
  • Continental US coverage at launch

Trust & safety

Built to protect every transaction

From your first swipe to package delivered, ReFind keeps buyers and sellers safe at every step.

🔒

Secure Payments

Every transaction runs through Stripe. Your card details never touch our servers — PCI-compliant, industry-standard encryption from checkout to payout.

🛡️

Buyer Protection

"Item not as described" disputes are always honored within 3 days of delivery. Our team steps in to make it right, regardless of the seller's return policy.

🚩

Reporting & Blocking

Flag suspicious listings or users in one tap. Block anyone instantly — bidirectional, so blocked users can't see your listings or contact you either.

⭐

Airbnb-Style Reviews

Buyers rate sellers across five dimensions — accuracy, shipping speed, packaging, communication, and overall value. Full transparency before every purchase.

🤖

Image Moderation

Every listing photo runs through automated AI moderation before going live. If the check fails, the image is rejected — not approved by default.

✅

Account Security

Email verification on sign-up, secure token-based auth, session timeouts, and brute-force protection — your account is yours alone.
