What Makes ReFind Different

We built the features that other resale platforms are missing.

👕👖👗
Discovery

Swipe Through Your Feed

Your feed is personalized to your style preferences, favorite categories, and size. Discovering your next favorite piece should feel effortless.

  • Swipe right to save to your Wishlist
  • Swipe up to add straight to Cart
  • Swipe left to pass — no pressure
  • Feed learns from your preferences over time
📏
Fit Matching

Know It Fits Before You Buy

Forget size charts that vary by brand. ReFind compares actual garment measurements to your body measurements and shows you what fits.

  • Sellers provide pit-to-pit, waist, hips, and length measurements
  • "Fits You" badge on listings within your measurement range
  • Toggle the fit filter to only see items that match
  • Dramatically reduces returns and disappointment
💰
Negotiation

Make an Offer, Get a Deal

See something you love but the price isn't right? Submit an offer. Sellers can accept, decline, or counter — all within the app.

  • Offer/counteroffer negotiation built into messaging
  • Buy Now if you want it at listed price
  • Transparent — no hidden fees or auction pressure
  • 13% platform fee only charged to sellers on completed sales
📦
Shipping

Prepaid Labels, No Hassle

Sellers can generate prepaid shipping labels right in the app. Buyers see exact shipping costs upfront — no surprises at checkout.

  • USPS, UPS, FedEx, and DHL supported via Shippo
  • Tracking updates pushed to both buyer and seller
  • Sellers can also arrange their own shipping
  • Continental US coverage to start

Trust & Safety

We take protecting buyers and sellers seriously.

🔒

Secure Payments

All payments processed through Stripe. We never store your card details. PCI-compliant from day one.

👥

Buyer Protection

"Item not as described" disputes are always honored, regardless of the seller's return policy. We've got your back.

🛡

Reporting & Blocking

Report harassment, spam, scams, or fake listings. Block users instantly — bidirectional, so blocked users can't see you either.

★

Airbnb-Style Reviews

Buyers rate sellers on accuracy, communication, shipping speed, packaging, and value — five categories, full transparency.

📷

Image Moderation

Every listing image runs through automated moderation. If the check fails or the service is unreachable, the image is rejected — not approved.

🔐

Account Security

Optional two-factor authentication, encrypted token storage, session timeouts, and brute-force protection built in.
